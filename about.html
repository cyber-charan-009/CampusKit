About CampusKit

CampusKit is a free collection of practical student utilities, built to make everyday academic planning simpler. Calculators and file tools run in your browser; predictor data is illustrative and should be checked against official counselling and exam sources.

Our approach

We aim for clear, fast, mobile-friendly tools with no account requirement. We do not guarantee admissions outcomes or exam results. Always verify decisions with the relevant institution.
