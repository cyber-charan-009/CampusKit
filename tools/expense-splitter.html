How it works & FAQ

Where are expenses stored?

They are stored in localStorage on this browser and remain on this device. Clearing browser site data removes them.

How is settlement calculated?

Expenses are split evenly across the roommate names entered. The tool creates transfers from net debtors to net creditors.

Disclaimer: This is a convenience calculator. Confirm amounts and settle directly with your roommates; records are local to this browser.
