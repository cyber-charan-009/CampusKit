How it works & 2027 Predictor FAQ

Are these official 2027 cutoffs?

No. Every course option is labeled 2027 Projected Cutoff. Where matching 2026 counselling AIR data exists, its exact closing rank is carried forward unchanged as the 2027 planning baseline. No percentage reduction is applied. The original source year, rank, round and seat pool remain preserved. If only older evidence or a planning assumption is available, the course explicitly identifies that basis; it is not described as a verified 2026 cutoff.

How is marks-to-AIR estimated?

The score lookup retains published 2025 score/AIR bands and scales them to a hypothetical 2.3–2.5 million candidate pool (2025 baseline: 2,209,318 appeared). NTA does not publish an exact rank for every possible score. Ties, paper difficulty and candidate performance can change the result substantially.

What do AIQ and SQ mean here?

For government colleges, AIQ means all-India/central counselling; SQ means state counselling subject to domicile and reservation rules. For private colleges, the open-route label means management/open counselling and does not imply a 15% MCC allocation. State government-quota fees in private colleges are kept separate from management fees. Unsupported state routes are excluded.

How do the category filters work?

Exact matching historical category AIR is used unchanged, with 2026 data preferred where available. Missing reserved-category references use illustrative UR multipliers (EWS 1.08, OBC 1.20, SC 1.85, ST 2.10) and are labeled category scenarios. Open/management assumptions do not invent caste advantages. Candidate category in an allotment source does not by itself establish a reserved-seat entitlement. Check current state subcategories, minority status, certificates and seat availability.

What about AFMC, CMC and St. John’s?

AFMC requires separate screening and medical fitness and has service conditions; AIR alone does not predict selection. CMC Vellore has state and restricted management/minority routes. St. John’s uses KEA counselling with institute-specific categories. Their illustrative anchors are not verified closing ranks. Check each institution’s current admission notice.

How should I interpret Safe, Moderate and Ambitious?

Safe means your rank is at least 15% better than the scenario closing AIR; Moderate spans the remaining range through 15% beyond it; Ambitious extends to 20% beyond it. These are scenario comparisons, not measured probabilities, admission guarantees or confirmation of a spot-round seat.

Where do fees, stipends and bonds come from?

The supplied college lists are preserved verbatim. The primary government/state/Cat-A annual fee is stored for each college; budget filters use the applicable route’s fee. Unknown management fees are excluded when a maximum budget is set. Hostel costs, deposits and discontinuation penalties may be additional. Confirm every amount before choice filling.
