How it works & 2027 Predictor FAQ

How is marks-to-AIR estimated?

CampusKit uses published 2025 score/AIR ranges and interpolates within each reported band. NTA does not publish an exact rank for every score; the estimate is year-specific and can vary due to ties.

Does MCC cover every state quota?

No. MCC data covers its AIQ/central/deemed counselling routes. State quota lists are published by separate state authorities. State-rank cutoff rows are kept on their own rank scale; currently the official Chhattisgarh OR-CR source is ingested for state-merit comparisons.

What chance badge will state quota get?

Only a state merit rank can be compared with a state merit cutoff. AIR rows and state-rank rows are labeled separately and never compared as if they shared one rank scale.

Are fees and college type verified?

College metadata is joined from the attributed NMC college list where names match. Fees remain blank without a comparable official fee source; selecting a fee limit excludes rows with unknown fees.

How are 2027 ranks and cutoffs handled?

The score-to-AIR lookup uses published 2025 score bands and a 2.3-2.5 million candidate planning scenario based on 2,209,318 appeared candidates in 2025. It is an estimate, not an NTA prediction. College records retain their published year, counselling round, and rank basis; available 2026 MCC records are Round 1 AIQ allotments.

How should I interpret the course chance?

Course chances are rank-versus-closing-rank heuristics, not calibrated admission probabilities or seat guarantees. AIQ AIR and state merit ranks are separate scales; non-comparable rows do not receive a numeric chance.

Sources: MCC UG archive, MCC 2026 Round 1 result, open-source 2025 NEET AIQ references, KEA UGNEET 2026 portal, NTA NEET UG, 2025 score/AIR analysis, Chhattisgarh 2025 OR-CR, and NMC college metadata source. Records are historical counseling outcomes, not allotment guarantees. Verify current rules with MCC and your state authority.
