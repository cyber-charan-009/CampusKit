How it works & FAQ

Are documents sent to a server?

No. PDF parsing and output generation happen in your browser using pdf-lib.

Why is the library loaded from a CDN?

The PDF feature uses a version-pinned public CDN script; internet access is needed to load that library.

Disclaimer: Keep your own backups and check generated documents before submitting. Password-protected or unusual PDFs may not process.
