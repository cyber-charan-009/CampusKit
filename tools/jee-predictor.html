How it works & 2027 Predictor FAQ

How do quota and category ranks work?

JoSAA publishes separate OR-CR rows by institute, program, quota, seat category, and gender pool. OPEN cutoffs use CRL; reserved and PwD seats use their published respective category rank. The predictor matches those same row dimensions.

How reliable is marks to percentile?

NTA does not publish a deterministic marks-to-percentile curve. CampusKit interpolates 2025 post-exam shift analysis and estimates AIR using the 2025 candidate pool; shifts and years differ.

What does the trend column mean?

Where a matching prior-year row exists, the displayed trend compares its closing rank with the year and round shown for the current cutoff. The 2024 data is a comparator; 2026 IIT JEE Advanced cutoffs use the linked round-wise open dataset, while other institute records retain the latest available year.

Are fees and placements included?

Fee and placement figures are shown only when present in a source. Cutoff files do not contain comparable institute-wide annual fees or average CTC, so unavailable values remain blank instead of being guessed. A fee limit excludes rows with unknown fees.

How are 2027 ranks and cutoffs handled?

JEE Main marks are mapped through estimated 2025 shift curves, then percentile is translated into a 2027 planning rank range using 1.45-1.55 million candidate scenarios. This is not an official NTA prediction. The dataset uses the latest available JoSAA cutoff row per course, including 2026 IIT Advanced rounds and older source years where 2026 records are unavailable.

How should I interpret the course chance?

Course chances are rank-versus-closing-rank heuristics for scanning choices, not calibrated admission probabilities or seat guarantees. Preparatory and non-comparable rank rows are labeled reference-only.

Sources: Official JoSAA OR-CR, Official JoSAA 2026 OR-CR page, 2026 IIT Advanced round-wise CSVs, attributed 2018–2025 CSV repository, and 2025 shift analysis. Historical rows and estimated score curves cannot guarantee an allotment. Always check official JoSAA rules and the current seat matrix.
