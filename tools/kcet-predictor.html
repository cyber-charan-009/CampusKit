How it works & 2027 Predictor FAQ

How does the KCET composite estimate work?

It averages the board PCM percentage with KCET marks converted to a percentage, then applies a broad historical rank heuristic. It is not KEA's official rank formula and should not be used as a guaranteed rank.

Which cutoff round is shown?

KEA 2026 Round 3 and COMEDK 2026 Round 3 cutoff documents are used where matching course and category rows are available; the latest matching 2025 record is retained when a 2026 row is absent. Each course card shows its published year and round.

What do 371J, rural, and Kannada-medium filters do?

KEA source columns distinguish HK seats and suffixes such as R and K. The page preserves the source categories and filters those columns directly; eligibility rules are set by KEA.

Why are fees blank?

Cutoff PDFs do not include each college's annual tuition consistently. Unverified fees are not guessed; selecting a max-fee filter excludes rows with unknown fees.

How are 2027 ranks and cutoffs handled?

The KCET composite estimate uses 50% board PCM and 50% normalized KCET marks, then a broad planning heuristic to estimate a rank range with an approximate +/-15% planning band. It is not KEA's official rank formula. Cutoff records retain the year, round, category, and quota published by KEA or COMEDK.

How should I interpret the course chance?

Course chances are rank-versus-closing-rank heuristics, not calibrated admission probabilities or seat guarantees. Confirm category, quota, special eligibility, and current-round rules with KEA / COMEDK.

Sources: KEA UGCET 2026 portal, COMEDK counselling 2026 documents, open-source KCET historical data, and the 2025 official sources retained where a matching 2026 cutoff is not published. Verify categories, special quotas, fees, and current-round rules with KEA / COMEDK.
