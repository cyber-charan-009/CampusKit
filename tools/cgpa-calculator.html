How it works & FAQ

Which conversion should I select?

Choose the method prescribed by your university or board. Rules may differ by program and academic year.

How is target SGPA calculated?

Under equal semester credits, required SGPA = target × (completed semesters + 1) − current CGPA × completed semesters.
