How it works & FAQ

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The image is decoded to a browser canvas and encoded locally on your device.

Will PNG transparency be preserved?

Output is JPEG, so transparent areas become white. Check the downloaded file against your application requirements.

Disclaimer: Target size is a best effort; image dimensions and visual quality affect compression. Review the final image and follow the portal's exact specifications.
