Privacy Policy

CampusKit tools are designed to work in your browser. Theme preference and roommate expense records may be saved in localStorage on your device. Image compression and PDF processing happen locally in your browser; selected files are not uploaded by this site.

Third-party services

The PDF utility loads pdf-lib from a public CDN. If advertising is introduced, third-party providers may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. This starter site currently contains only reserved ad spaces.

Your choices

You can clear local site data from your browser settings. For privacy questions, visit the contact page.
